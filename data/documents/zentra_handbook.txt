ZENTRA LABS EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK

1. About the company
Zentra Labs was founded in 2019 in Pune by Meera Iyer. The head office is in Pune, and the company has a small second office in Hyderabad. Employees chat on an internal tool called Zentra Talk.

2. Working hours
The standard working hours are 10:00 to 18:30, Monday to Friday. Everyone must be available during the core hours of 11:00 to 16:00. Friday afternoons are meeting-free so that people can focus on deep work.

3. Leave policy
Every employee gets 24 days of paid leave per year and 8 days of sick leave per year. Unused paid leave can be carried forward, but only up to 6 days. Maternity leave is 26 weeks and paternity leave is 10 days.

4. Remote work
Employees may work from home up to 3 days per week. Wednesday is the mandatory office day for every team. New joiners must work from the office for their first 2 weeks.

5. Expenses and travel
Expense claims must be submitted within 21 days of the expense. During business travel the meal limit is Rs 900 per day. Any single claim above Rs 10,000 needs approval from a director. Flights must be booked at least 7 days in advance.

6. Learning and equipment
Each employee gets a learning budget of Rs 40,000 per year for courses and books. Laptops are replaced every 3 years. A one-time home-office allowance of Rs 15,000 is paid to every new employee.

7. Probation and notice
The probation period is 4 months. The notice period is 30 days during probation and 60 days after confirmation.

8. Information security
Passwords must be changed every 90 days and the company VPN is mandatory when working outside the office. Any security incident must be reported to the security team within 2 hours. Personal USB drives are not allowed on company laptops.

9. Engineering practices
Every code change needs 2 approvals before it can be merged. Engineers take turns on the on-call rotation, which changes every week. The on-call allowance is Rs 3,000 per week.

10. Benefits and referrals
Health insurance covers the employee and their family up to Rs 5 lakh. A referral bonus of Rs 25,000 is paid after the referred person completes 6 months at the company.
